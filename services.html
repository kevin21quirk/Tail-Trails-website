01Dog walking

Dog walking

Every walk is planned around your dog's energy, sociability and favourite sniffing spots. Groups are small and carefully matched — never more than a few dogs at a time. Typical walk length is 45–90 minutes, on foot, using local parks, woods and safe neighbourhood paths. Morning, afternoon and full-day options available.

02Day care

Day care adventures

Tails & Trails is more than a walk. Your dog spends the day with someone who genuinely loves their company — exploring, resting and getting the attention they deserve. Alyssia can collect your dog on foot or meet locally, keeping routines simple. Days are outdoor-led with plenty of fresh air, exercise and supervised play.

03Feeding & home visits

Feeding & home visits

If your dog needs a meal, a top-up of water or a calm check-in during the day, we can build feeding into the visit — exactly to your instructions. Same portions, same bowls, same timing. Available as regular or one-off help to fit your life.
