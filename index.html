What we do

A full day of tail-wagging care

From pick-up at your front door to the final happy photo, Tracks & Trails by Alyssia is built around the things your dog loves most — fresh air, fun and company that feels like family.

Walks made for sniffing, exploring & playing

Every outing is tailored to your dog's pace and personality — whether that's a slow sniffari or a park play session with friends.

Walks run from 45 minutes to a full morning out, collected on foot from your front door. Groups are kept small and carefully matched so every dog gets the attention they deserve — and comes home genuinely tired and happy.

Routes weave through local parks, woodland paths and quiet neighbourhood streets — the kind of varied terrain that keeps curious noses busy from start to finish.

  • ✦Small, matched groups — never more than a few dogs at a time so yours gets real attention
  • ✦Photo updates every walk — bright, happy snaps sent straight to you so you never miss a moment
  • ✦Flexible timing — morning, afternoon and full-day slots to fit around your schedule
  • ✦Locally on foot — no car, no crates, just a friendly face at the door and straight out on the trail
